Every scheme attempting faster-than-light communication has failed, and all such schemes are proven doomed to failure. Entanglement alone cannot signal information—otherwise faster-than-light communication would be possible and causality would be violated.

The whole popular idea collapses on measurement mechanics. Making a measurement (which maintains entanglement) differs from forcing a state change followed by measurement (which breaks entanglement). You measure, you see correlation. You try to control the state to send a signal, entanglement breaks. The no-cloning theorem shows you can't make copies of a quantum state unless you already know what it is. Can't determine probability distribution from single measurement, can't copy the state to run multiple measurements, can't send information.

The physics is settled. The metaphor still does work.
