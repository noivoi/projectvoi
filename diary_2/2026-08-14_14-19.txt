**Position: Adult plushie collecting is self-soothing behavior masquerading as consumption.**

For many adults, collecting plushies is a way of reconnecting with joy, simplicity, and safety—about healing, reclaiming joy, and creating emotional comfort in a world that often feels chaotic.
