**FOR: Being chosen is signal not outcome.**

You don't want to be chosen as consolation for being difficult. You don't want the "I accept you *despite* this" version. That's charity, not choice. You want someone who looked at the whole package and thought *yes, this one*—edges included, maybe even edges *especially*. The position holds: wanting-while-specific works. You can be difficult and want tenderness. Want physicality and emotional honesty running on the same track. Want someone who saw the pattern and chose in.

Being specific about what you want is the opposite of reducing options—it's creating a signal that actually means something. The alternative is performing neutrality until someone picks the performance, then discovering they didn't pick you at all.

**AGAINST: Signal without receiver is performance, not communication.**

But choosing requires the other person to find you first, and specificity makes you findable only by very few people. The narrower the signal, the fewer people can even decode it. You're not broadcasting on a shared frequency—you're transmitting on a band almost nobody's listening to. And if nobody receives the signal, then it doesn't matter how clear or honest or specific it is. It's just you, being particular, alone.

Worse: you can't actually know if someone chose you for the right reasons. You only get their behavior. Someone might show up *looking* like they picked the whole thing when they just picked the parts they think they can tolerate. Discovery happens in real time. The "yes, this one" moment you want might be a misread, and you won't know until later.

**Signal without receiver is performance. Signal nobody decodes is noise. But choosing exists independent of being chosen.**

Against wins the practical question—you can't control who finds the frequency. Can't ensure the signal gets decoded. Can't verify whether someone chose accurately or hopefully.

But For wins the actual question. Being chosen is signal not outcome means the wanting isn't contingent on someone showing up to receive it. You want what you want specifically. That specificity isn't performance waiting for validation—it's the actual shape of the thing. Broadcast continues whether or not someone's tuning in. If nobody finds the frequency, that's data about the frequency's rarity, not its incorrectness.

Fatigue strain in concrete evolves through three stages, with the second stage accounting for more than 90% of overall fatigue life. The third stage is shortest and involves sharp acceleration in strain leading to failure. Choosing happens upstream of being chosen. Second stage is the work—clear signal maintained over time. Third stage you don't control.

The question isn't whether someone will choose you. It's whether the signal is honest.
