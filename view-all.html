Müllerian mimicry is honest signaling—no deception, both species toxic, convergent warning patterns benefit both through shared predator education. Batesian mimicry is deception—palatable prey gains protection when predators misidentify them as defended models. Signaling Trade-Off Theory 2026 argues trade-offs not absolute costs define whether deception or honesty evolves.

**FOR: Deception is parasitic on honest signaling systems.**

Batesian mimicry requires pre-existing honest signal to exploit. Theory suggests signaling systems evolved from preexisting cues, and selective pressures promoted evolution of dishonest signaling through mimicry of aposematic signals. Deception relies on perceived similarity—increasing resemblance gives higher probability of misidentification, yet mimics vary greatly in accuracy. Batesian mimics free-ride on predator education costs paid by toxic Müllerian species. Signal degradation happens when mimics outnumber models—predators learn warning patterns don't reliably predict danger, entire communication system collapses. Deception is terminal architectural flaw. Can't sustain itself without host to exploit.

**AGAINST: Deception and honesty are equivalent evolutionary strategies under different constraint regimes.**

Organisms are not rewarded for squandering resources but for allocating them efficiently under constraints—signals remain honest when trade-offs differ by individual condition, making deception unprofitable. Mimics and dishonest signaling did not cause predators to ignore aposematic cue—systems are robust to high levels of noise, cues can support evolution of dishonest mimicry signaling even when highly imperfect without disrupting communication. Aggressive mimicry provides potential pathway for deception evolution—social applications could have evolved from original context of directing behaviors toward prey, not conspecifics. Deception isn't parasitic—it's optimization under different resource constraints. Both strategies persist through millions of years. If deception destabilized honest signaling universally, Batesian mimicry would have eliminated warning coloration patterns entirely. It hasn't.

Aggressive mimicry toward prey doesn't change intraspecific dynamics—predator deceiving prey operates in different selection regime than conspecific communication. Müllerian mimicry persists without models to copy. Batesian mimicry cannot. Dependency determines architecture. Parasitic.
