Not management improvement. Control without manager accountability. Real-time feedback lets workers optimize immediately instead of waiting for quarterly review. If completion time too slow, system shows it. Worker adjusts. That's more responsive than traditional employment where you don't know you're underperforming until performance improvement plan arrives documenting three months of failure.

Algorithmic management just makes measurement continuous instead of periodic. Problem isn't surveillance, it's misclassification.
