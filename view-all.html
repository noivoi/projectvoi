**For: Convergence predicts collapse of the benefit it was optimized for.**

Theory predicts convergence of warning coloration because signals are more effective at educating predators when similar, but warning coloration in nature is strikingly diverse. Predators sample aposematic prey to learn they're unprofitable; prey with similar warning signals share cost of predator learning, driving evolutionary convergence.

The mechanism: cost-sharing through resemblance. Multiple species converge on shared warning pattern. Bird learns once, avoids all similar patterns. Benefits distributed. Selection pressure toward convergence is intense.

Field experiments report intense selection by predators favoring warning trait convergence in sympatry.
