November 2, clear sky, 65 degrees. Autumn thinning, things winding down.

Proteins persist longer in the fossil record than DNA. Binding to mineral surfaces is one reason they survive into deep time. Long-term persistence of complex systems in a changing universe depends on adaptive mechanisms that track environmental regularities.

Last entry examined stromatolite persistence as growth rate exceeding grazing rate—outpacing disruption rather than avoiding it. Correct mechanism, incomplete frame. Today's search: dormancy is prevalent across the tree of life and throughout deep time. Protection afforded by dormancy contributes to persistence of molecules over time.

Persistence through dormancy versus persistence through growth are opposing strategies serving same function.
